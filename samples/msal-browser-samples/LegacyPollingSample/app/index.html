MSAL.js Legacy Polling Sample

Demonstrates the 1P-only system.enableLegacyPolling opt-in. When enabled, popup, logoutPopup, and ssoSilent flows poll the popup/iframe URL for the auth response (v4 behavior) instead of relying on COOP-safe redirect-bridge messaging. Use this when your host (e.g. Office Add-ins, legacy in-app browsers) cannot adopt COOP yet.
